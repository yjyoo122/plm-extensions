FUSION MANAGE UX  -  READ THIS FIRST

1. Unzip this folder anywhere you like, for example your Documents folder.
2. Double-click "Start.cmd". Please use Start.cmd for this very first start.
3. A black window opens. That window IS the app running, so please leave it open.
4. Your web browser opens by itself after a few seconds.
5. The first time only, the browser asks you a few setup questions. Answer them and click Save.
6. The app restarts by itself and you are ready to work.
7. To stop the app, close the black window.
8. After that first start, the icon "Fusion Manage UX" works too, and you can drag it to your desktop.
9. Nothing is installed on your computer and no administrator rights are needed.
